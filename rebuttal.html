R4 · reviewer eSo2

What delay each method is charged in simulation

In simulation the policy latency and cache age are set by a fixed cost model from GR00T-N1.7 — VLM latency dvlm = 0.75d₀ and denoising-step latency ddn = 0.25d₀, where the unit delay d₀ is the cost of GR00T’s default 4 denoising steps — not by how many denoising steps the simulation network runs. Flow and RTC run 15 steps on Leap Cube and up to 5 on Kinetix for quality, but are charged only GR00T’s 4, which favors them. A method is excluded at a given d₀ when its charged policy latency exceeds H/2 (H = 16 on Leap Cube, H = 8 on Kinetix), as it then no longer allows continuous real-time execution.
